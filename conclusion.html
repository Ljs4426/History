Unit 8 Pursuing American Ideals

Conclusion

What do these three lessons actually add up to?

The part of this unit I found most interesting was the Supreme Court cases. Mahanoy specifically — I didn't know there had never been a ruling on off-campus social media speech before 2021, and that case directly affects what I can post. The Reagan stuff was important to understand because every debate about taxes or the national debt today starts from what he put in place in 1981, whether people know it or not. The Cold War lesson surprised me the most because I went in thinking it was a finished chapter and came out realizing it never really closed. NATO expansion, the INF Treaty dying in 2019, Ukraine in 2022 — all of it traces back to decisions made when the Wall fell. What I took from all three lessons is that history doesn't stay in the past. It keeps showing up in different forms, and the only difference between someone who understands what's happening and someone who doesn't is whether they know where it started.
