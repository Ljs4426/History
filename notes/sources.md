## Lesson 55: A Shift to the Right Under Reagan

1. **Tax Policy Center** — "Historical Highest Marginal Income Tax Rates"
   Urban Institute & Brookings Institution, 2023.
   <https://www.taxpolicycenter.org/statistics/historical-highest-marginal-income-tax-rates>
   *Used for: top marginal rate history showing the 1981–1986 rate cuts.*

2. **Pew Research Center** — "Trends in U.S. Income and Wealth Inequality"
   January 9, 2020.
   <https://www.pewresearch.org/social-trends/2020/01/09/trends-in-income-and-wealth-inequality/>
   *Used for: Gini coefficient and wealth share data showing post-1980 inequality growth.*

3. **Congressional Budget Office** — "The Long-Term Budget Outlook"
   Updated annually.
   <https://www.cbo.gov/topics/budget>
   *Used for: current national debt figures and projection charts.*

4. **Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED)** — Historical economic data
   <https://fred.stlouisfed.org>
   *Used for: GDP growth rates and S&P 500 historical data during Reagan years.*

   Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, 1997.
   <https://www.fdic.gov/bank/historical/history/>
   *Used for: S&L crisis cost estimates ($124 billion taxpayer bailout figure).*

## Lesson 56: Ending the Cold War

1. **Council on Foreign Relations** — "NATO's Trajectory"
   Backgrounder series, updated 2024.
   <https://www.cfr.org/backgrounder/north-atlantic-treaty-organization-nato>
   *Used for: NATO membership timeline showing post-1991 expansion to 14+ new members.*

2. **Mearsheimer, John J.** — "The Causes and Consequences of the Ukraine War"
   *Foreign Affairs*, May/June 2022.
   <https://www.foreignaffairs.com/articles/russia-fsu/2022-04-12/causes-and-consequences-ukraine-war>
   *Used for: ICED citation on NATO expansion and Russia's stated security concerns.*

3. **Brookings Institution** — Russia/Ukraine analysis series, 2022–2024.
   <https://www.brookings.edu/topic/russia/>
   *Used for: context on how Russian government frames NATO expansion in current rhetoric.*

   <https://www.reaganlibrary.gov/reagans/reagan-administration/reagan-foreign-policy>

5. **National Security Archive, George Washington University** — "The INF Treaty and Its Aftermath"
   <https://nsarchive.gwu.edu>
   *Used for: declassified documents on Cold War arms negotiations.*

## Lesson 59: Moving Forward — Debating America's Founding Ideals

1. **Mahanoy Area School District v. B.L.**, 594 U.S. ___ (2021)
   Full opinion (PDF): <https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/20pdf/20-255_g3bi.pdf>
   *Used for: ICED citation — Breyer majority, "diminished" school authority over off-campus speech.*

2. **ACLU** — "Mahanoy Area School District v. B.L. Case Page"
   <https://www.aclu.org/cases/mahanoy-area-school-district-v-bl>
   *Used for: legal strategy framing and why this case mattered for student rights.*

3. **Bostock v. Clayton County, Georgia**, 590 U.S. 644 (2020)
   Full opinion: <https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/19pdf/17-1618_hfci.pdf>
   *Used for: Title VII / LGBTQ+ employment protection ruling, Gorsuch majority opinion.*

4. **Rucho v. Common Cause**, 588 U.S. 684 (2019)
   Full opinion: <https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/18pdf/18-422_9ol1.pdf>
   *Used for: federal courts cannot hear partisan gerrymandering cases; state-level doors remain open.*

5. **Brennan Center for Justice** — "Redistricting Commissions"
   <https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/research-reports/redistricting-commissions>
   *Used for: state-level redistricting commission adoption post-Rucho.*

6. **Tinker v. Des Moines Independent Community School District**, 393 U.S. 503 (1969)
   (Background case for Lesson 59 Example 1)
   *Used for: the foundational student speech precedent that Mahanoy builds on.*
